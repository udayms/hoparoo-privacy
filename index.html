Timeroo Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026
The short version: Timeroo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Everything you create in the app — routines, tasks, activity history, and settings — stays on your device.

Timeroo ("the app") is a routine-timer app designed for families. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

What data the app stores, and where

  • Routines, tasks, and activity history you create are stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This data never leaves your device automatically.
  • App settings (sounds, theme, accent color) are also stored locally on your device.
  • Timeroo has no backend server. There is no account creation, no sign-in, and no user database anywhere.

Backup and restore

Timeroo lets you export a backup file of your routines and history, and later restore from one. This is entirely user-initiated:

  • When you back up, the app creates a file and hands it to your device's own share sheet (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Files, AirDrop, email, or wherever you choose to save it). Timeroo itself never uploads this file anywhere.
  • When you restore, you pick a file from your device using the system file picker. Timeroo reads only the file you select.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Timeroo does not use any analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies of any kind. There are no third-party services integrated into the app that receive your data.

Permissions

Timeroo may request only the device permissions strictly required for features you use (for example, access to the Files app when you choose to restore a backup). The app does not request microphone, camera, location, or contacts access.

Children's privacy

Timeroo is designed to be used by children under parental supervision, alongside a parent or guardian. Because the app collects no personal information and has no account system, no personal information is collected from children of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Since Timeroo has no accounts or contact information stored, we recommend checking this page directly for updates.
